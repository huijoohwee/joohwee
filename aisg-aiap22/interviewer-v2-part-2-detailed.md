## Scoring Calibration Notes

### Converting Q&A Performance to Numerical Scores

**Per-Question Scoring** (0-10 scale):
- **0-3 (Poor)**: Cannot answer, fundamental misunderstanding, defensive
- **4-6 (Qualified)**: Correct but surface-level, missing key insights, incomplete reasoning
- **7-8 (Good)**: Solid answer with reasoning, addresses core question, some depth
- **9-10 (Excellent)**: Comprehensive, demonstrates mastery, connects concepts, addresses follow-ups proactively

**Dimension Aggregation**:
- Technical Depth (Q1.1-1.3): Average of 3 questions → weighted 40% of Q&A → 20% of overall
- Business Reasoning (Q2.1-2.3): Average of 3 questions → weighted 30% of Q&A → 15% of overall
- Problem-Solving (Q3.1-3.2): Average of 2 questions → weighted 20% of Q&A → 10% of overall
- Communication (Q4.1): Single question → weighted 10% of Q&A → 5% of overall

**Red Flags (Auto-Fail Criteria)**:
- Cannot derive gradient descent update rule AND defensive about gap → Technical depth insufficient
- Consistently dismisses stakeholder concerns (product/legal/compliance) → Poor collaboration fit
- Blames submission gaps on external factors ("I didn't have time") without taking ownership → Fixed mindset

**Green Flags (Strong Hire Signals)**:
- Derives gradient descent fluently + Bayesian interpretation → Graduate-level ML theory
- Quantifies business impact for every technical decision → Strong business acumen
- Proposes creative solutions to multi-constraint scenarios → Strategic problem-solver
- Uses "Explain Like I'm 5" effectively without prompting → Natural communicator
