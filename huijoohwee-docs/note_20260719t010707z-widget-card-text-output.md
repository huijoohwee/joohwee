Below is a comprehensive financial plan for a restaurant startup in Malaysia. It covers key components, assumptions, projected financial statements, funding needs, and milestone timelines. Adapt figures to your specific concept, location, and market research.

1) Executive assumptions (example inputs to tailor)
- Concept: Full-service casual dining or fast-casual with local Malaysian flavors (or a specific cuisine).
- Location: Tier-1 city (e.g., Kuala Lumpur, Penang) or Tier-2 city; high foot traffic area (regional mall, busy street).
- Seating capacity: 40–60 seats for a small to mid-sized restaurant.
- Opening speed: 6–9 months from securing capital to opening.
- Hours: 11:00–22:00 daily (adjust for lunch/dinner demand).
- Revenue mix: Dine-in 70%, take-out 20%, deliveries 10%.
- Average spend per guest: RM 40–70 (adjust for concept; include beverages, desserts, etc.).
- Gross margin: 60–70% food, 65–75% beverages, mix depending on menu.
- Staffing: General manager, assistant manager, chefs/c cooks, kitchen crew, service staff, bar/coffee, cleaners, POS/IT.
- Rent: 8%–12% of revenue (typical for Malaysia in good locations).
- License/permits: business license, food handling, halal/certifications if applicable.
- Financing: mix of owner equity, bank loan, vendor financing, and/or angel investment.
- tax: Malaysian corporate tax rates; GST/SST considerations (note: SST reintroduced variants; verify current regime).

2) Startup cost (capital expenditure)
Assumptions:
- Leasehold improvements (kitchen, dining area, lighting, branding): RM 180,000 – RM 350,000
- Furniture, fixtures, equipment (FF&E): RM 120,000 – RM 250,000
- Kitchen equipment (stove, hood, refrigeration, dishwasher): RM 180,000 – RM 320,000
- Signage and branding: RM 20,000 – RM 50,000
- POS system, software, hardware: RM 20,000 – RM 40,000
- Initial inventory (food & beverage): RM 60,000 – RM 100,000
- Security deposit and pre-opening costs: RM 40,000 – RM 80,000
- Marketing and launch (grand opening): RM 20,000 – RM 50,000
- Working capital (3–6 months): RM 120,000 – RM 180,000
Total estimated capex: RM 660,000 – RM 1,420,000

3) Ongoing monthly operating costs (example)
- Rent (prime location): RM 12,000 – RM 40,000
- Utilities (electricity, water, gas, internet): RM 6,000 – RM 12,000
- Payroll (include EPF, SOCSO, bonuses): RM 25,000 – RM 60,000
- Cost of goods sold (food and beverages): 28%–36% of revenue (baseline)
- Marketing and promotions: RM 3,000 – RM 12,000
- Licenses, insurance, POS fees, maintenance: RM 2,000 – RM 6,000
- Cleaning and waste disposal: RM 2,000 – RM 4,000
- misc/contingency: RM 2,000 – RM 5,000
Total monthly operating costs: RM 52,000 – RM 151,000 (varies by size and location)

4) Revenue projections (monthly, sample for a ~50-seat concept)
Assumptions:
- Average spend: RM 50 per guest
- Guests per day: 70–150 (varying by season and marketing)
- Days open: 28–30

Example:
- Monthly diners: 2,000 – 4,000
- Monthly revenue: RM 100,000 – RM 200,000
- Food cost: RM 28,000 – RM 72,000 (28%–36% of revenue)
- Beverage cost: RM 8,000 – RM 18,000 (8%–9% of revenue)
- Gross profit: RM 64,000 – RM 110,000
- Gross margin: ~60%–70%

5) Profitability and break-even
- Break-even revenue = fixed costs / gross margin
- Example: Fixed monthly costs (rent + payroll + utilities + admin) ≈ RM 60,000 – RM 110,000
